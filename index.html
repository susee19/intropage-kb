Just a Girl Who Loves Shiny Metals 💫

Meet Kalyani Bansal, a fresher from Hyderabad who's landed in NIT Trichy to master the art of Metallurgy and Materials Engineering – because clearly, normal subjects weren’t challenging enough. She's got Hyderabadi spice, metallurgical precision, and enough spark to run a welding lab solo. Navigating campus like a pro (well… unless Google Maps disagrees), she’s already making people wonder if she's part of the curriculum—because everyone's trying to study her. With 8:30 AM classes testing her patience , Kalyani’s on a mission to shine brighter than a freshly polished alloy. Smart, chill, and just the right amount of mysterious – she might just be the rare element NITT didn’t know it needed.
